Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery focused on creating fresh breads, pastries, and cakes for local customers, families, commuters, and special events.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe quality baked goods begin with carefully selected ingredients. We aim to use fresh ingredients and prepare our products with attention and care.

Our Team

Our staff works together to prepare fresh baked goods, assist customers with pre-orders, and create a welcoming experience for everyone who visits the bakery.

Behind the Scenes

Our bakery team prepares products throughout the day so customers can enjoy fresh breads, pastries, and cakes.
